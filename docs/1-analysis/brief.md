# Project Brief: Deiwala Plastic Products Website

## Executive Summary
A professional product showcase website for Deiwala Plastic Products, designed to display their catalog of PET and HDPE containers and convert traffic into business inquiries.

## Problem Statement
Industrial and retail buyers in the plastic packaging market lack a centralized, technical catalog for Deiwala's products, leading to manual inquiry overhead and missed digital opportunities.

## Proposed Solution
A modern, high-performance web catalog built with Next.js that categorizes products by material and volume, featuring a direct-to-email inquiry system.

## Target Users
* **Industrial Procurement Officers:** Looking for specific neck sizes and volumes for large-scale production.
* **Small Business Owners:** Seeking reliable packaging for local food or beverage products.

## MVP Scope
* Home, About, and Contact pages.
* Dynamic product catalog (PET Jars, PET Bottles, HDPE Jars, HDPE Bottles).
* Automated inquiry form integrated with Resend API.

## Success Metrics
* Successful delivery of lead data to `deiwalag04@gmail.com`.
* 100% accuracy of technical specifications (Volume, Neck Size) in the catalog.
